Why We Started Kindly

A personal note from our co-founders

For over 15 years, we’ve built businesses in the Philippines centered around one principle: frontline teams perform at their absolute best when they are given continuous training, clear operational dignity, and direct alignment with the client’s success.

When enterprise companies started scaling AI operations, we noticed a recurring flaw in traditional outsourcing: client teams were either stuck managing faceless, high-turnover crowd-worker platforms or locked into rigid, traditional BPO seat contracts that cared more about headcount than output quality.

We created Kindly to bridge that exact gap. We combine Manila's top 1% data talent with tight operational feedback loops, giving software companies a managed operational team that feels like an extension of their own internal product floor.
