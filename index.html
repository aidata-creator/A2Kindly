Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started

How do you guarantee quality with a distributed workforce?

Every unit passes multi-layer QA: gold-standard calibration tasks, double-blind sampling, and dedicated QA analysts at roughly one per fifteen specialists. Accuracy rates appear on a weekly dashboard and SLAs carry credits. With per-output pricing, defective units are never billed.

How do you handle data security and privacy?

Client data is accessed through controlled environments with role-based access and NDA-bound specialists. For sensitive engagements we use virtual desktop setups where data never leaves your infrastructure. We work under your data processing agreement (DPA) and build toward ISO 27001 standards.

What about time zones?

Output-based work is asynchronous: you send volume, we return QA'd results against a turnaround SLA. For real-time coverage we staff to your business hours. The Philippines overlaps naturally with Australia and Asia, and we run dedicated shifts for US and European hours.

Why the Philippines?

The Philippines is the world's premier outsourcing hub, combining exceptional English fluency, deep Western cultural alignment, and high AI tool adoption rates.

Isn't fair pay going to make you more expensive?

All-in rates are competitive with any high-quality Philippine provider, typically well below onshore equivalents. Fair pay reduces attrition, retraining, and error rates—giving you a stable team that retains context rather than high turnover.
