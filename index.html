Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How do you guarantee quality with a distributed workforce?

Every unit passes multi-layer QA: gold-standard calibration tasks, double-blind sampling, and dedicated QA analysts. Accuracy rates appear on a weekly dashboard, and defective units are never billed under per-output pricing.

How do you handle data security and privacy?

Client data is accessed through controlled environments with role-based access and NDA-bound specialists. For sensitive engagements, we support virtual desktop infrastructure (VDI) and execute custom Data Processing Agreements (DPAs).

What about time zones?

Output-based work is asynchronous: you send volume, we return QA'd results against a turnaround SLA. For real-time coverage, we run dedicated shifts matching US, European, and APAC hours.

Why the Philippines?

The Philippines offers an experienced outsourcing workforce with exceptional English fluency, Western business culture familiarity, and high AI tool adoption rates.

Isn't fair pay going to make you more expensive?

Fair pay reduces attrition and error rates, giving you a stable team that maintains context. Combined with our AI speedups, overall operating costs remain lower than traditional BPOs.
