Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you guarantee quality with a distributed workforce?

Every unit passes multi-layer QA: gold-standard calibration tasks, double-blind sampling, and dedicated QA analysts at roughly one per fifteen specialists. Accuracy rates appear on a weekly dashboard and SLAs carry credits. With per-output pricing, defective units are never billed.

How do you handle data security and privacy?

Client data is accessed through controlled environments with role-based access and NDA-bound specialists. For sensitive engagements we use virtual desktop setups where data never leaves your infrastructure.

What about time zones?

Output-based work is asynchronous: you send volume, we return QA'd results against a turnaround SLA. For real-time coverage we staff to your business hours.
